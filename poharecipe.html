POHA

Poha is a popular, quick-cooking Indian dish made from flattened rice. It is light, nutritious, and commonly eaten for breakfast in India.

Ingredients

  • Poha (Flattened Rice)
  • Oil
  • Onions
  • Peanuts
  • Turmeric
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Wash the poha and drain excess water.
  2. Heat oil and add peanuts.
  3. Add onions and spices.
  4. Add poha, mix well, and cook.
